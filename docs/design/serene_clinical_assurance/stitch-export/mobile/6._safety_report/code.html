Combiflam Ibuprofen + Paracetamol
info

Taking Aspirin and Ibuprofen together greatly raises the danger of severe stomach bleeding and ulcers. It also stops Aspirin from protecting the heart.

upload_file Why this happens (Medical Insight):

Ibuprofen blocks the antiplatelet effect of low-dose cardiac aspirin when taken together, reducing cardio-protection while multiplying gastrointestinal irritation threefold in elderly patients.
